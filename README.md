# CHAM ROBOTICS

### Universal Humanoid Intelligence · Physics · Spatial Computing

**Researching the next generation of physically intelligent humanoid systems.**

CHARM ROBOTICS is an independent robotics research and development initiative focused on humanoid physics, whole-body control, real-time simulation, and intelligent 3D environments.

Our goal is to explore the connection between physical simulation, artificial intelligence, and human-like motion — from research models to interactive real-time systems.

---

## Research Domains

### 01. HUMANOID ROBOTICS

**Whole-Body Control & Dynamic Locomotion**

Research into physically grounded humanoid movement and autonomous body control.

- Whole-body balance and posture control
- Center of Mass (CoM) regulation
- Dynamic locomotion and foot placement
- Push recovery and disturbance rejection
- Fall management and get-up control
- Full-body inverse kinematics
- Active ragdoll and physics-driven motion

### 02. UNIVERSAL HUMANOID SIMULATION

**Bridging Robotics Physics and Web-Based Avatars**

Exploring ways to connect humanoid robot control systems with real-time 3D character environments.

- MuJoCo-based robot simulation research
- Three.js real-time simulation
- VRM humanoid visual representation
- Rapier rigid-body physics
- Full-body IK and motion integration
- Robot model and visual-skin integration
- Physics-driven animation systems

### 03. ROBOT LEARNING & CONTROL

**Simulation-to-Control Research**

Investigating the integration of robot simulation, control policies, and intelligent movement systems.

- Policy-driven locomotion research
- Motion planning and control architecture
- Hierarchical whole-body control
- Physics-based validation
- Robot model integration
- Simulation-to-web deployment

### 04. SPATIAL COMPUTING

**Real-Time 3D Geometry & Spatial Transformation**

Research into real-time spatial computation and advanced 3D environments.

- Signed Distance Fields (SDF)
- Real-time spatial distortion
- Raymarching and geometric transformation
- Non-Euclidean spatial concepts
- Interactive 3D environments

## Featured Research

### Universal Humanoid Control

Developing a research framework for humanoid balance, locomotion, whole-body coordination, and recovery behavior.

### VRM × Physics Integration

Exploring the use of VRM characters as visual representations within physics-driven humanoid simulation environments.

### MuJoCo × Web Simulation

Investigating how robot models, simulation environments, and control policies can be connected to interactive browser-based systems.

### Real-Time Spatial Computing

Researching geometric transformations and spatial effects for interactive 3D environments.

## Research Philosophy

> Intelligence should not only generate motion.
> It should understand the physical consequences of motion.

We are interested in systems where perception, control, physics, and motion operate as a unified architecture.
